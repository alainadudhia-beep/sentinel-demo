Client: Perennial Capital (sell-side PE, vendor)
Target: TalentBridge Group (specialist recruitment, technology & digital, UK + Ireland)
Engagement type: Vendor Due Diligence
Sector: Business Services / Recruitment

COMMERCIAL TERMS
Planned duration: 5 weeks
Actual duration: 5 weeks
Planned fee: £175k
Actual fee: £175k
Team: 1 Partner, 1 Manager, 2 Consultants, 1 Analyst

SCOPE
UK technology and digital recruitment market, TalentBridge competitive positioning, revenue quality (perm vs contract, repeat customer analysis), candidate supply dynamics, key individual dependency risk, growth strategy assessment.

No scope changes.

EXECUTION NOTES
A well-run engagement. Perennial are experienced VDD clients and had prepared the management team thoroughly — TalentBridge came to the kick-off with a pre-prepared data pack that covered most of our standard requirements. This saved approximately 3 days of data chasing.

Revenue quality was the analytical focus. TalentBridge had 68% contract revenue (good quality, recurring) and 32% perm — the mix was shifting toward contract, which Perennial wanted to evidence. We were able to validate the trend credibly.

Candidate supply analysis in tech recruitment is always challenging post-2021 given talent market dynamics. We were careful to caveat our supply analysis appropriately, noting that 2021-22 was an abnormal period.

One interesting dynamic: TalentBridge had three billing managers who collectively accounted for 55% of revenue. This was flagged to Perennial early and they agreed to build retention packages into the sale structure.

Expert calls: 6 completed. Technology recruitment market contacts.

OUTCOME
VDD completed on schedule. Sale process ran to plan. Final deal completed with key individual retention packages as standard.

KEY LESSONS
- Billing manager dependency in recruitment businesses is almost always present — flag early, allow time for Perennial to build into deal structure
- Pre-prepared data packs from experienced PE-backed management teams save significant time; acknowledge and reward this dynamic
- Recruitment VDDs during 2021-23 require careful caveating of any candidate supply analysis given market distortion

RISK FLAGS (materialised)
- Key person risk (MEDIUM): Three billing managers = 55% revenue. Addressed through deal structure.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Clean engagement, on time and on budget. Good template for specialist recruitment VDDs.
