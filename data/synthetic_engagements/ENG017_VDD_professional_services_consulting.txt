Planned duration: 4 weeks

Actual fee: £160k

SCOPE
UK management consulting market (infrastructure and public sector segment), Meridian competitive positioning vs Arcadis, Mott MacDonald, and the Big 4, revenue quality (project-based vs framework contract), client concentration and retention, partner/senior consultant dependency analysis, growth pipeline credibility.

Scope addition (week 1): Greystone requested a view on Meridian's framework contract pipeline — several large government frameworks were due for renewal in the next 18 months, which was a known risk. Incorporated into the revenue quality workstream without extending scope.

EXECUTION NOTES
A well-run engagement with a few notable dynamics.

Consulting firm VDDs are meta — there's an inevitable self-consciousness about consultants assessing consultants, and Meridian's senior leadership were acutely aware of the process. They were cooperative but occasionally challenged our methodology in sessions, which the team handled well.

Revenue quality analysis required careful treatment. Meridian's revenue was a mix of framework contract drawdown (predictable, lower margin), bespoke project work (higher margin, less predictable), and a small but growing advisory retainer book. We built a three-stream revenue model which Greystone found helpful.

Framework contract renewal risk was the key issue. Two frameworks (representing c.35% of revenue) were up for renewal within 18 months. Meridian's win rate on framework re-bids had been 100% historically, but one of the frameworks was being expanded in scope, meaning new competitors could join. We flagged this as a medium-high risk rather than low risk, which Greystone accepted with some pushback.

Expert calls: 6 completed. Public sector procurement specialists, ex-infrastructure consulting MD, framework contract specialist.

OUTCOME
Deal completed. Recommended proceed with conditions (framework renewal risk flagged as a warranty item). Sale process ran well. Greystone very pleased with the work product.

KEY LESSONS
- Professional services firms are self-aware subjects; expect methodology challenges from management in sessions — prepare for it
- Framework contract renewal risk in public sector consulting can be systematically assessed; don't treat as unknowable
- Three-stream revenue model (contract drawdown, project, retainer) is a useful structure for professional services VDDs
- "100% historical win rate" on re-bids is not a forward-looking assurance; always assess the forward competitive environment

RISK FLAGS (materialised)
- Market risk (MEDIUM): Framework contract renewal risk flagged; incorporated into deal structure.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Clean delivery on time and budget. Strong work product. Good client. Greystone confirmed they would use us on future processes.
