Client: Lumio (consumer tech startup, smart lighting, pre-Series A)
Type: Founder strategy — go-to-market
Engagement type: Strategy
Sector: Consumer Technology

COMMERCIAL TERMS
Planned duration: 2 weeks
Actual duration: 2 weeks
Planned fee: £75k
Actual fee: £75k
Team: 1 Manager, 1 Consultant, 1 Analyst (no Partner lead; Partner review only)

SCOPE
Go-to-market strategy for UK launch. Scope: channel prioritisation (retail vs DTC vs trade/B2B), launch messaging framework, early customer acquisition strategy, and 12-month revenue model.

No scope changes.

EXECUTION NOTES
A lean, fast engagement. Lumio was a well-funded startup with a clear product but an underspecified GTM. The founding team were technical and product-oriented; the commercial strategy was their genuine gap.

The engagement ran to schedule. The Manager ran the project with limited Partner involvement (appropriate for the scope and fee level). The team was lean and worked efficiently.

Key analytical insight: Lumio's initial assumption was a direct DTC-first approach. The analysis showed that DTC customer acquisition costs in the smart home category were prohibitively high for a pre-brand business, and that a trade/installer channel would provide faster, lower-CAC volume. The founders were initially resistant but accepted the logic quickly when we modelled the CAC comparison directly.

The 12-month revenue model was built conservatively, using the trade channel as the primary growth driver with DTC as a secondary brand-building exercise. The founders used this directly in their Series A pitch deck.

No expert calls needed for this scope — desk research and team sector knowledge sufficient.

OUTCOME
Strategy delivered. Founders satisfied. Lumio used the GTM framework for their Series A, which closed 4 months later. Good reference engagement.

KEY LESSONS
- Consumer tech DTC CAC economics are frequently underestimated by technical founders; make this the first analytical question
- Short strategy engagements for startups are viable and good for relationship building; accept them selectively based on growth potential
- Trade/installer channel is underused as a launch route for smart home hardware; build it into default GTM options

RISK FLAGS
None.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Clean, efficient engagement. Strong impact for a small mandate. Good relationship foundation with founders — follow-on potential as they scale.
