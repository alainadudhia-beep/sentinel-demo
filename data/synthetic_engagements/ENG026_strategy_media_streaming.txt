Planned duration: 5 weeks

Actual fee: £200k

SCOPE
Strategic options assessment for Channel 9's transition from linear broadcast to streaming/digital. Scope: UK streaming market dynamics and competitive intensity, Channel 9's audience and content strengths, build vs. partner vs. white-label platform options, revenue model scenarios (AVOD vs. SVOD vs. hybrid), and board recommendation.

Scope addition (week 3): Board member requested inclusion of a licensing and content syndication revenue assessment — not in original brief but directly relevant to the strategic options and sensible to add. Absorbed without timeline impact.

EXECUTION NOTES
A well-run and intellectually interesting engagement. The Channel 9 mandate came from a board that was genuinely uncertain about the right strategic direction and wanted an independent, expert view. This is the best kind of strategy mandate — open questions, genuine uncertainty, board-level engagement.

The UK streaming market context required careful framing. The "peak streaming" narrative was emerging in mid-2022 and we had to address it directly in the market dynamics chapter. We were careful to distinguish between SVOD saturation (real) and AVOD/hybrid growth (still strong), which was the critical nuance for Channel 9's options.

The build vs. partner vs. white-label analysis was the strategic core. Our recommendation was white-label platform (Brightcove or equivalent) + AVOD model + content syndication. The board found this helpful and it was consistent with what they had instinctively sensed but couldn't confirm.

Content strengths analysis was the most qualitative workstream and required careful methodology — we combined audience data with qualitative assessment of content positioning, which was a somewhat subjective exercise. We were explicit about this.

Expert calls: 7 completed. Streaming platform operators, media M&A advisers, content syndication specialist, ex-ITV digital lead.

OUTCOME
Board accepted the recommendation. Channel 9 is in vendor selection for the white-label platform. Good outcome.

KEY LESSONS
- Streaming market strategy work requires a nuanced treatment of SVOD vs. AVOD dynamics — don't conflate "peak streaming" narrative with the full market picture
- Build vs. partner vs. white-label is a well-established strategic choice framework for media businesses; use it explicitly
- Boards that are genuinely uncertain make the best strategy clients — they engage with the analysis rather than looking for confirmation

RISK FLAGS
None materialised.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Clean engagement. Strong analytical work. Good board engagement. Good outcome. Good template for media strategy mandates.
