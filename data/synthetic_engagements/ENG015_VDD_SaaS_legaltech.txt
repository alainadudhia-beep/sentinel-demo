PROJECT CLOSURE NOTE
Engagement Reference: ENG-015
Date: July 2023
Prepared by: Engagement Manager

CLIENT & MANDATE
Client: Lantern Growth (sell-side PE, vendor)
Target: CaseCraft (legal workflow and matter management SaaS, UK mid-market law firms)
Engagement type: Vendor Due Diligence
Sector: Technology / SaaS / Legal

COMMERCIAL TERMS
Planned duration: 5 weeks
Actual duration: 5 weeks
Planned fee: £180k
Actual fee: £180k
Team: 1 Partner, 1 Manager, 2 Consultants, 1 Analyst

SCOPE
UK legal tech market (case management, document automation, billing management), CaseCraft ARR quality and growth analysis, competitive positioning vs Clio, LEAP, and legacy on-premise vendors, customer retention and expansion analysis, implementation and switching cost assessment.

Scope addition (week 2): Lantern asked for a section on CaseCraft's AI product roadmap in the context of LLM disruption risk — this was becoming a standard bidder question in legal tech processes. Added a chapter, absorbed within the timeline. This was good scope to add; it came up in every subsequent bidder question list.

EXECUTION NOTES
Well-run engagement with one interesting complication.

CaseCraft's product was technically strong and the management team were impressive. The CEO had a background in both law and software, and understood buyer questions better than almost any management team we'd worked with. This made sessions very efficient.

The complication: CaseCraft operated in a genuinely disrupted competitive environment. Several new AI-native legal workflow tools had launched in 2022-23, and one of them (a US-backed competitor) had just announced UK market entry. This required us to update our competitive analysis twice during the engagement as new information emerged, which was managed but required agility.

Implementation and switching cost analysis required detailed customer interviews — CaseCraft had unusually strong switching moat but it was not well-documented by management. We conducted 8 customer interviews (agreed as an add-on in initial scoping) which provided the qualitative evidence needed to support this claim credibly.

ARR reconciliation was clean. NRR of 112% validated.

Expert calls: 6 completed, plus 8 customer interviews.

OUTCOME
VDD completed. Sale process ran competitively with multiple bidders. CaseCraft attracted strong interest partly because the VDD gave bidders confidence on the switching cost question — that was the key value debate in the process.

KEY LESSONS
- AI disruption risk sections are now standard in any SaaS vertical with clear AI applicability — include by default in VDD scope
- Customer interviews for switching cost evidence are worth the investment in high-churn-risk markets; plan for 6-8 by default
- Competitive landscape updates during an engagement are becoming more common in fast-moving tech categories; build flex time into the competitive analysis workstream

RISK FLAGS (materialised)
- Competitive environment (MEDIUM): New AI-native entrant announced UK entry mid-engagement; required analysis updates.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Clean delivery. Strong work product. Good outcome for client. VDD added demonstrable value in the sale process.
