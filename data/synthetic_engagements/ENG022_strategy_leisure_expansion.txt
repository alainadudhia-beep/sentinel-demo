Engagement Reference: ENG-022
Date: January 2024
Prepared by: Engagement Manager

CLIENT & MANDATE
Client: Aqua Leisure Holdings (PE-backed, 18 sites, indoor leisure/swimming)
Type: Growth strategy mandate — PE portfolio support
Engagement type: Strategy
Sector: Leisure

COMMERCIAL TERMS
Planned duration: 4 weeks
Actual duration: 4 weeks
Planned fee: £160k
Actual fee: £160k
Team: 1 Partner, 1 Manager, 2 Consultants, 1 Analyst

SCOPE
Estate expansion strategy: identification and prioritisation of new site locations for Aqua's rollout plan over 3 years, demand modelling framework, competitive catchment analysis, site assessment criteria, and partnership model options (landlord JV vs owned vs leased).

No scope changes.

EXECUTION NOTES
Clean engagement. The mandate was well-defined from the outset — Aqua's PE sponsor (Redwood Growth, a repeat client) had a clear thesis and wanted rigorous analytical support for the site selection framework.

Demand modelling for leisure rollout is a well-established methodology and the team had done similar work previously. We built a catchment demand model using MOSAIC data, competitor proximity, and demographic segmentation that Aqua found directly usable.

Management were collaborative and brought strong operational context — they knew their current sites well and had strong instincts about the characteristics of high-performing catchments. Our job was to formalise and test those instincts rather than build from scratch.

One area of productive challenge: management believed that new site payback was 4 years. Our modelling suggested the payback at current build costs was closer to 5.5-6 years at base case. This was a meaningful difference for the investment thesis. We worked through the assumptions with the CFO and arrived at an agreed model; management accepted the revised payback assumption.

Expert calls: 4 completed. Leisure property specialists, ex-David Lloyd estates director.

OUTCOME
Strategy delivered and accepted. Site expansion prioritisation shortlist of 8 locations delivered. Aqua have since opened 2 of the 8 and are in heads of terms on a third.

KEY LESSONS
- Leisure site rollout demand modelling benefits from MOSAIC + competitor proximity combination — standard approach that works well
- Management's operational intuition about their own estate is usually reliable; the job is to formalise it, not replace it
- PE portfolio strategy engagements are efficient when the sponsor has a clear thesis; align with sponsor before kick-off, not just with management

RISK FLAGS
None materialised.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Very clean engagement. Good outcome. Good template for leisure estate expansion strategy work.
