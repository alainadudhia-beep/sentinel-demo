PROJECT CLOSURE NOTE
Engagement Reference: ENG-002
Date: September 2022
Prepared by: Engagement Manager

CLIENT & MANDATE
Client: Redwood Growth Equity
Target: PeopleFirst (HR tech SaaS, mid-market UK & Ireland)
Engagement type: Commercial Due Diligence
Sector: Technology / SaaS

COMMERCIAL TERMS
Planned duration: 2 weeks
Actual duration: 2 weeks
Planned fee: £95k
Actual fee: £95k
Team: 1 Partner, 1 Manager, 1 Consultant, 1 Analyst

SCOPE
Fast-turn CDD on a HR tech SaaS asset. Scope: TAM/SAM for UK mid-market HR software, competitive moat assessment (vs Personio, BambooHR, HiBob), churn analysis and net revenue retention, ICP validation. No customer survey due to timeline — expert calls and desk research only.

EXECUTION NOTES
This was a compressed two-week engagement and it ran exactly as planned. Redwood are efficient clients — they'd already done significant work themselves before bringing us in and largely wanted a credibility check and a few areas of independent validation. That framing was helpful; we knew what we needed to deliver and what we didn't.

No scope changes. Management at PeopleFirst were accessible and clearly well-prepared for the DD process. Data quality was high — they had a well-structured Salesforce instance and provided clean cohort data without prompting.

One minor friction point: the Partner on their side was clearly running the process to a tight deadline (signing was imminent) which created some pressure to soften findings around churn in the SMB segment. We held the line on the characterisation, which the client accepted after a brief discussion.

Expert calls: 4 completed (CHRO contacts at mid-market companies, one ex-Personio product lead).

OUTCOME
Deal completed. Recommended proceed. NRR was genuinely strong (118%) and the ICP was well-defined. The SMB churn issue was flagged as a condition to monitor post-acquisition.

KEY LESSONS
- Two-week CDDs on SaaS are achievable if the client has done pre-work and the data room is clean; don't discount them
- Watch for timeline pressure creating subtle pressure to soften findings — name it clearly in the team pre-mortem
- Redwood are fast, professional clients; good for repeat work

RISK FLAGS (materialised)
None significant.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Clean engagement, good outcome. Under-resourced slightly (could have used a second consultant) but team performed well given the constraint.
