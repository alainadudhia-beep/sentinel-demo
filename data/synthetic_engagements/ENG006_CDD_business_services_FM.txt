Client: Carver Partners

COMMERCIAL TERMS
Planned duration: 3 weeks

Actual fee: £115k
Team: 1 Partner, 1 Manager, 1 Consultant, 1 Analyst

SCOPE
UK outsourced FM market assessment (post-COVID structural trends, IFM vs bundled services dynamics), Brightside competitive positioning and contract quality assessment, customer concentration analysis, pipeline and renewal profile review.

No scope changes.

EXECUTION NOTES
Textbook engagement. Carver were straightforward clients — this was their third FM sector deal in two years and they knew the category well. They largely wanted our independent view on market growth assumptions and a sanity check on Brightside's customer retention narrative.

Brightside's data was well-organised. Contract data was clean; we were able to build a proper renewal waterfall and weighted revenue retention model in week 1. Management were professional and clearly used to the DD process — this was their second PE-backed sale.

The main analytical challenge was the IFM (integrated FM) vs bundled services question — market is bifurcating and Brightside sits in an awkward middle position. We developed a clear positioning matrix that Carver found useful.

Expert calls: 5 completed (facilities directors, ex-CBRE FM lead, procurement specialist). Good quality.

OUTCOME
Deal proceeded. Clean proceed recommendation. Strong contract retention metrics, defensible market position, experienced management.

KEY LESSONS
- FM sector CDDs benefit from a renewal waterfall model built early — it organises everything else
- Second-time PE-backed management teams are generally well-prepared; factor this into time allocation for management sessions
- Carver are reliable, low-drama clients

RISK FLAGS
None materialised.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Very clean engagement. Good to use as a benchmark for scope and timeline on similar business services CDDs.
